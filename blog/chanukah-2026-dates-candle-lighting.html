Chanukah 2026 Dates: When to Light Each Candle

Chanukah 5787 begins at sundown on Friday, 4 December 2026 and ends at nightfall on Saturday, 12 December 2026. It's an unusual year because the first and the eighth candles both fall on a Friday night, so families light before Shabbat twice.

For all eight nights you need 44 candles in total (36 plus a shamash each night).

Lighting on Friday night

On Friday the menorah is lit before the Shabbat candles, because once Shabbat begins you can't light it. The candles must burn until at least half an hour after nightfall, and the small standard Chanukah candles often don't last that long. Use thicker candles or oil, and light a few minutes before Shabbat candle-lighting time.

Lighting on Saturday night

Wait until Shabbat is over. Communities differ on whether Havdalah or the menorah comes first, so follow your family's custom or ask your rabbi.

Rosh Chodesh Tevet

Rosh Chodesh Tevet falls on Thursday 10 and Friday 11 December, starting Wednesday evening. Thursday is also Chag HaBanot, a traditional celebration of women in many Sephardi communities.

The blessings

Each night two blessings are said before lighting: l'hadlik ner Chanukah and she'asah nisim. On the first night only, Shehecheyanu is added.
